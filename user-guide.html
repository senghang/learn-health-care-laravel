Logging In

Starting a New Visit (Triage)

  1. Click OPD → Triage or the "New Visit" button on the Dashboard.

  2. Search for the patient by name or code. If new, register them first (Patients → Register).

  3. Select Visit Type: OPD for outpatient, IPD for inpatient admission.

Creating an Invoice

  1. Go to Billing → Invoices → New Invoice, or use OPD Workflow Step 10 (Invoice).

  2. Search and select the patient. Enter a Visit Code if applicable.

  3. Select Payment Type: CASH / HEF / NSSF / CARD.

  4. Add Services from the catalogue (consultation fees, procedures, etc.).

  5. Add Medicines dispensed to the patient.

  6. Apply a Discount or Tax if applicable. The total auto-updates.

  7. Click Create Invoice. A code like INV-00287 is assigned. Status is Pending.

Collecting Payment

  1. Open the invoice and click "Collect Payment".

  2. Enter the Amount received (can be partial).

  3. Select Payment Method:

    CASH HEF (Health Equity Fund) NSSF CARD BAKONG

  4. Enter a Reference Number for non-cash payments (card/transfer).

  5. Click Record Payment. If amount = remaining balance → status changes to Paid. If partial → Partial.

ℹ️ Multiple payments can be recorded against one invoice (partial payments). The invoice shows a running balance: Total − Paid = Balance due. Each payment is logged individually with method, reference, and collector name.

Section 8
🏪 Pharmacy & Dispensing
Dispensing medicines to OPD and IPD patients
OPD Dispensing Queue

  1. Go to Pharmacy → Dispensing. All prescriptions with status null or Partial are listed.

  2. Click a prescription row to open the dispensing detail view.

  3. Review each medicine line — current stock level is shown next to each item. Items with insufficient stock are highlighted.

  4. Click "Dispense All" to mark the entire prescription as Dispensed. Stock is automatically deducted for every medicine line.

  5. Or set status to Partial if some items are out of stock — note which items were given.

⚠️ Setting status to Dispensed immediately deducts stock from the inventory. This action creates stock movement records. Ensure stock was received via Stock In before dispensing.
IPD Medication Administration

IPD medication orders are managed separately from the OPD dispensing queue. For admitted patients:

  1. Open the admission record in IPD → Admissions.

  2. Under Medication Orders, the nurse clicks "Administer" next to each dose when given to the patient.

  3. Stock is deducted at the time the medication order is created (not at administration).

Viewing the Medicine Catalogue

Go to Pharmacy → Medicines to browse all registered medicines. Filter by name, category, or stock status (low stock, out of stock). Click the ledger icon to view the full stock history of any medicine.

Section 9
📦 Inventory Management
Receiving stock, adjustments, and reporting
Stock In — Receiving Stock

  1. Go to Inventory → Stock Movements and click the Stock In tab.

  2. Select the medicine from the dropdown. Enter the Quantity received.

  3. Set the type: In (new purchase) or Return (patient/supplier return).

  4. Optionally fill in: Supplier name, Reference No., Unit Cost, Expiry Date, Batch No.

  5. Click Save. The medicine stock increases and a movement record is created.

Stock Out — Manual Removal

  1. Click the Stock Out tab.

  2. Select medicine, enter quantity, choose type: Out (issue/disposal) or Expired.

  3. Enter a reason note. Click Save.

ℹ️ Stock Out is for manual removals only. Stock deducted through prescription dispensing or IPD medication orders is recorded automatically — do not double-count these in Stock Out.
Physical Count Adjustment

  1. Click the Adjustment tab (or go to Inventory → Stock Movements → Adjustment).

  2. Select the medicine. Enter the actual counted quantity from the physical count.

  3. The system computes the delta (difference) and records a movement showing the adjustment direction (+ or −).

  4. Enter a reason (e.g. "Monthly stock count"). Click Save.

Stock Movements Ledger

Go to Inventory → Stock Movements (main view) to see a chronological log of all stock movements across all medicines. Filter by movement type, medicine name, or date. Counters show total quantities per movement type.

Stock Balance Report

Go to Inventory → Stock Balance for a summary of all medicines with current stock, alert level, stock value, and status. Medicines at or below alert level are highlighted in orange. Out-of-stock items are shown in red.

Per-Medicine Ledger

From Pharmacy → Medicines, click the ledger icon (📒) on any medicine row to see its complete movement history: every stock in, stock out, dispense, adjustment, and the running balance after each movement.

💡 Set a Stock Alert Level when creating a medicine. The dashboard will flag the medicine when stock reaches this level, giving your team time to reorder before running out.

Treatment Orders

  1. Open the admission record from IPD → Admissions and click the admission code.

  2. Under Treatment Orders, click "+ Add Treatment".

  3. Fill in: Treatment Type (Procedure / Nursing Care / Diet / Therapy) · Name (required) · Instructions · Frequency · Route · Duration · Notes.

  4. Treatments are created with status Ordered and are automatically marked Completed upon discharge.

Medication Orders (IPD)

  1. Under Medication Orders, click "+ Add Medication".

  2. Select a medicine from the clinic stock. Stock availability is validated — an error is shown if stock is insufficient.

  3. Enter: Dose · Route · Frequency · Quantity · Start Date · End Date · Notes.

  4. Stock is immediately deducted when the medication order is saved (not when administered).

  5. Ward nurses click "Administer" on each dose to record the administration time and staff member.

Bed Transfer

  1. Click "Transfer Bed" on the admission detail page.

  2. Select the new bed from available beds. Click Confirm Transfer.

  3. The old bed is automatically freed (status → Cleaning). Ward, room, and bed are updated on the admission record.

Discharging a Patient

  1. Click "Discharge" on the admission detail page.

  2. Enter: Discharge Type (Cured / Improved / Against Advice / Transferred / Deceased / Other) · Discharge Summary · Patient Condition · Visit Outcome · Discharge Date/Time.

  3. Click Confirm Discharge. The following happens automatically:

    • Admission status → Discharged
    • Bed freed → status → Cleaning, patient pointers cleared
    • Encounter closed → status → Completed
    • All active treatments → Completed
    • All active IPD medications → Completed
    • Parent visit updated

🔑 Users, Roles & Permissions
Managing login accounts and access control
Creating a User Account

  1. Go to Employee → Users → New User.

  2. Enter: Name, Email (login credential), Phone.

  3. Assign a Role from the dropdown.

  4. Optionally link to an Employee record.

  5. Set a password: minimum 8 characters, must include uppercase, lowercase, and numbers.

  6. Set Active status (inactive users cannot log in).

  7. Click Create User.

Managing Roles

Go to System → Roles & Permissions. Each role is defined by a name, optional description, and a Level (0–100, higher = more authority in the hierarchy).

⚠️ System roles (built-in) are protected — they cannot be modified or deleted. Custom roles you create are fully manageable. A role cannot be deleted if it has users assigned to it.

Section 14
🛠️ Troubleshooting
Common issues and how to resolve them
ProblemCauseSolution
Menu item is missing from sidebar Role lacks required permission Ask an administrator to add the relevant permission to your role in System → Roles & Permissions.
Medicine not found in prescription auto-complete Medicine not created or set to inactive Add or activate the medicine in Pharmacy → Medicines or Settings → Medicines.
Cannot dispense — stock insufficient Stock is zero or less than required quantity Receive stock first via Inventory → Stock Movements → Stock In before dispensing.
IPD medication order fails Stock insufficient for the ordered quantity Same as above — ensure stock is available before placing the IPD medication order. Stock is deducted at order creation.
Cannot edit invoice Invoice is in Paid or Void status Paid and void invoices are locked. Only Pending invoices can be edited.
Invoice balance appears wrong Outdated page / calculation Refresh the page. The balance is calculated as Total − sum of all recorded payments. Check if a payment was applied to the wrong invoice.
Bed still shows Occupied after discharge Discharge not completed via IPD module Beds are freed automatically when discharge is processed through IPD → Admissions → Discharge. Ensure the full discharge form was submitted.
Cannot delete a role Role has users assigned to it, or it is a system role Reassign users to a different role first. System roles can never be deleted.
Patient search returns no results Searching with incorrect field Search by surname, given name, patient code (e.g. PT-00142), or phone number. Partial matches work.
Language toggle not working Browser cookie/session issue Click the language flag button in the top navigation bar. If it persists, clear browser cookies and log in again.
Workflow step shows wrong progress Steps saved out of order Progress tracks completed + skipped steps out of 10. Revisit any step and click Save to mark it as completed.
Permissions were not seeded New clinic setup or module added Go to System → Roles & Permissions and click "Seed Default Permissions". Safe to run multiple times.

⚡ Common Workflow Quick Reference

🔵 New OPD Visit (complete cycle)

  1. Register patient (if new) → Patients → Register
  2. Start visit → OPD → Triage
  3. Complete steps 1–10 (Registration through Invoice)
  4. Collect payment → Billing → Invoices
  5. Dispense medicines → Pharmacy → Dispensing

🏥 New IPD Admission (complete cycle)

  1. Triage with Visit Type = IPD
  2. Admit patient → assign Ward & Bed
  3. Add treatment & medication orders daily
  4. Nurses record administration per dose
  5. Discharge → fill summary → bed auto-freed
  6. Generate invoice and collect payment
