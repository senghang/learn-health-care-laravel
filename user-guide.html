Section 5
💊 Prescriptions
Create prescriptions and track dispensing
5.1 Create Prescription

  1. Select patient from the prescription form.

  2. Add one or more medicines using the search field.

  3. Define dosage for each medicine line.

  4. Click Save — prescription is sent to the pharmacy queue.

💡 Prescriptions created inside OPD Workflow Step 7 are automatically linked to the patient visit. For repeat prescriptions, go to OPD → Prescriptions → New.
5.2 Medicine Lines
FieldDescription
Medicine NameSearch from the medicine catalogue
DoseAmount per administration (e.g. 1 tablet, 5ml)
FrequencyMorning / Afternoon / Evening / Night doses per slot
DurationNumber of days for the course
5.3 Dispensing

  1. Pharmacist opens Pharmacy → Dispensing.

  2. Reviews each medicine line and verifies stock is available.

  3. Clicks "Dispense All" — stock is deducted and prescription is marked Dispensed.

StatusMeaningStock Effect
PendingNot yet dispensedNone
PartialSome medicines given, others pendingNone (manual)
DispensedAll medicines fully given to patientStock auto-deducted

6.1 Create Order

  1. Create from OPD workflow Step 5 (linked to visit) or directly from Lab & Imaging → New Order.

  2. Select the required tests (lab) or modality (imaging: X-ray / Ultrasound / CT).

  3. Set urgency: Normal / Urgent / STAT.

6.2 Sample Collection

  1. Lab technician opens the order and clicks "Collect Sample".

  2. Select sample type: Blood / Urine / Swab / other.

  3. Status updates to Collected — collection time and staff are recorded automatically.

6.3 Results Entry

  1. Click "Enter Results" on the order.

  2. Enter results: numeric values, text findings, or interpretation notes.

  3. Attach supporting files (PDF reports or images) if available.

  4. Status updates to Processing.

6.4 Report Approval

  1. Doctor opens the completed results in Lab & Imaging → Results.

  2. Reviews findings and adds clinical notes.

  3. Clicks "Verify" to give final sign-off. Status → Completed.

  4. Results are now visible in the patient's full profile.

7.1 Create Invoice

All amounts are in USD ($) by default.

  1. Go to Billing → Invoices → New Invoice or use OPD Workflow Step 9.

8.2 Dispensing Process

  1. Go to Pharmacy → Dispensing. Pending prescriptions are listed here.

  2. Click a prescription to open the detail view.

  3. Validate that stock is sufficient for each medicine line (highlighted if low).

  4. Click "Dispense All" → medicine is given to patient, inventory is deducted automatically.

⚠️ Dispensing immediately deducts stock. Ensure stock was received in Inventory → Stock In before dispensing.
8.3 IPD Medication

  1. Doctor creates medication orders in IPD → Admissions → Medication Orders. Stock is deducted at order creation.

  2. Nurse opens the admission record and clicks "Administer" next to each dose when given.

  3. Administration time and nurse name are logged automatically.

Section 9
📦 Inventory Management
Stock in, stock out, adjustments, and reports
9.1 Stock In

  1. Go to Inventory → Stock Movements → Stock In.

  2. Select medicine, enter quantity received.

  3. Enter supplier name and reference number for accountability.

  4. Record expiry date and batch number if applicable.

  5. Click Save — stock increases and a movement record is created.

9.2 Stock Out

  1. Go to Inventory → Stock Movements → Stock Out.

  2. Select medicine, enter quantity, choose reason:

TypeWhen to Use
DispensedMedicine issued directly (outside normal prescription flow)
Damaged / ExpiredWrite-off damaged or expired stock
ℹ️ Do not manually record stock out for prescription dispensing — that is deducted automatically by the system.
9.3 Adjustments

  1. Go to Inventory → Stock Movements → Adjustment.

  2. Enter the actual physical count for a medicine.

  3. System calculates the difference (+ or −) and records a correction entry.

  4. Enter a reason note (e.g. "Monthly count — May 2026"). All corrections are logged for audit.

9.4 Reports
ReportWhere
Low stock alertsDashboard KPI card + Inventory → Stock Balance (orange items)
Expiry reportInventory → Stock Balance (filter by expiry date)
Usage analyticsInventory → Stock Movements (filter by date range and medicine)

10.1 Admission

  1. Select the patient (existing registration required).

  2. Assign room & bed from available beds.

  3. Enter admission notes: reason, attending doctor, admission type (Elective / Emergency / Transfer).

  4. Click Admit Patient. Admission record (e.g. ADM-00012) is created.

10.2 Treatment

👨‍⚕️ Daily Doctor Rounds

  • Add treatment orders: Procedure / Diet / Therapy
  • Update medication orders as needed
  • Review lab and imaging results

🩺 Nursing Care Logs

  • Record vital signs each shift
  • Log medication administration per dose
  • Document nursing care activities
10.3 Transfers

  1. Click "Transfer Bed" on the admission detail page.

  2. Select a new available bed (ward transfer or bed change).

  3. Confirm — old bed is freed automatically, new bed is updated on the admission record.

10.4 Discharge

  1. Click "Discharge" on the admission record.

  2. Complete the summary report: diagnosis, treatment summary, patient condition at discharge.

  3. Issue final billing for all services, medicines, and procedures during the stay.

  4. Provide medication plan (take-home medicines) if required.

  5. Click Confirm Discharge. Bed is freed automatically, admission is closed.

Discharge Types

  • Cured
  • Improved
  • Against Medical Advice
  • Transferred
  • Deceased

Section 12
🔑 Users, Roles & Permissions
Create login accounts, assign roles, configure access
12.1 Users

  1. Go to Settings → Users → New User.

  2. Enter: Name, Email (login credential), Password.

  3. Assign a Role from the dropdown.

  4. Set status to Active (inactive users cannot log in).

  5. Click Create User.

ℹ️ Password rules: minimum 8 characters, must include uppercase, lowercase, and a number.
12.2 Roles
RoleDefault Access Level
AdminFull access — all modules, all actions
DoctorOPD, IPD, prescriptions, lab & imaging
NurseTriage, vitals, IPD nursing care
PharmacistPharmacy dispensing, inventory
CashierBilling and payments only
⚠️ Built-in system roles cannot be deleted. A role with active users assigned cannot be deleted — reassign users first.

⚡ Quick Reference — Complete Workflows

🔵 New OPD Visit (10 Steps)

  1. Register patient (if new)
  2. Create OPD Visit — assign doctor
  3. Triage — vitals & severity
  4. Doctor consultation — diagnosis & plan
  5. Lab request (if needed)
  6. Imaging request (if needed)
  7. Prescription — medicines & dosage
  8. Pharmacy — validate & dispense
  9. Billing — add services & medicines
  10. Payment in USD → mark visit completed

🏥 IPD Admission (Complete Cycle)

  1. Register patient & assign room/bed
  2. Doctor adds treatment & medication orders
  3. Nurses log vitals & administer doses daily
  4. Lab & imaging ordered as needed
  5. Transfer bed if required
  6. Doctor completes discharge summary
  7. Generate final invoice — collect payment in USD
